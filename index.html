How to Play
  • Ni²+ ions fall from the top of the simulation box. Catch them!
  • Select a ligand (tap, click or press 1–5), then tap a Ni²+ to coordinate it.
  • Each Ni²+ needs 6 ligands to form a stable octahedral complex.
  • HSAB matching: match hard Ni²+ with hard bases (H₂O, Cl⁻), soft Ni²+ with soft bases (CN⁻, en) for bonuses.
  • Strong-field ligands (CN⁻, en) give CFSE bonuses. Homoleptic complexes (all same ligand) earn big bonuses.
  • Don't let Ni²+ reach the bottom — you lose research grants (lives)!
  • Transition states (‡) appear occasionally — tap them rapidly to optimize the saddle point before they decompose. Big rewards!
  • Tap Pause (or press P). Tap Run DFT for a computational chemistry power-up.

Ligands: H₂O (hard, weak field) NH₃ (borderline) Cl⁻ (hard, weak field) en (soft, strong field) CN⁻ (soft, strong field)
